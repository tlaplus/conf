            TLA+ Conf 2027
            Tuesday, April 6, 2027
            Washington, DC, USA

            TLA+ Conference 2027

            TLA+ conference brings together industrial and academic users of the TLA+ specification language and its associated tools, with a stronger focus on applying TLA+ in industry. Talks should present work of interest to users of TLA+ or PlusCal, such as but not limited to:

            • Use of TLA+ and its tools in industry
            • Integrating TLA+ with AI for automated reasoning
            • Novel tools and techniques leveraging TLA+ and its toolchain
            • Adopting TLA+ and its combination with other engineering practices
            • How you can participate and contribute

            Please send a 1-2 page abstract summarizing the content of a 30- to 45-minute presentation by January 15, 2027 to «tla2027» \o «@» \o «tlapl.us». Notification of acceptance will be sent by January 29, 2027. There will not be formal proceedings, but recordings will be made available on the web. Presentations of relevant work published elsewhere are welcome.

            Program committee:

            • Stephan Merz, Inria
            • Igor Konnov, independent
            • Murat Demirbas, MongoDB Research
            • Markus Kuppe, Oracle
            • Dmitry Kulagin, NVIDIA

            When & Where?

            TLA+ Conf will be an in-person event at The LINE Hotel in Washington, DC, on Tuesday, April 6, 2027, co-located with Antithesis BugBash 2027 (April 6–8 at the same hotel). The program runs 9:00–17:00 (EDT), including lunch and morning and afternoon coffee breaks.

            A standalone TLA+ Conf ticket will be available, and attendance can also be bundled with BugBash. Registration details will be posted on this page.

            Code of Conduct

            TLA+ Conf agrees with and follows the Linux Foundation’s Code of Conduct.
